Privacy policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Nova IPTV+ ("the app") is a media player. It does not provide, host or recommend channels, movies, series or playlists: users add their own. This policy explains what data the app handles and which services it talks to.

1. Who is responsible

The app is published by the developer InteractiveMedia. For any privacy question write to ragnarok59000.n26@gmail.com.

2. Data we collect

None. Nova IPTV+ has no user accounts and no servers of its own that receive your data, and it contains no advertising, analytics or trackers. The developer does not receive your playlist, your credentials, your history or your activity.

3. Data stored on your device

  • The addresses and credentials of the playlists you add (stored encrypted with a key protected by the Android system).
  • Your favourites, history, resume points, preferences and any downloads you make.
  • This data stays on your device, is excluded from cloud backups, and is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

4. Third-party services the app talks to

To work, the app makes requests to these services. Each can see your IP address and the specific request, and has its own privacy policy:

  • Your playlist provider (IPTV): receives your credentials and the catalogue and playback requests. The app does not control this service.
  • TMDB (themoviedb.org): receives the title and year of a movie or series to fetch cast photos, age rating, where to watch and trailers. "Where to watch" data comes from JustWatch.
  • OMDb (omdbapi.com): receives the title and year to fetch ratings and synopsis.
  • YouTube (Google): trailers play in YouTube's embedded player, which may process data under Google's policy.
  • OpenSubtitles (opensubtitles.com): if you search for online subtitles, it receives the title and the chosen language.
  • Google Cast / Google Play services: if you cast to a compatible device.

5. Security

Credentials are stored encrypted, automatic backups are disabled and the app checks that it has not been tampered with. No system is infallible; we recommend using unique passwords with your provider.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum legal age in your country) and collects no data from anyone. Parental control with a PIN is a local aid and does not replace adult supervision.

7. Your rights and how to delete your data

Because we do not collect personal data, there is nothing to request from the developer. To delete everything the app stores, remove your playlists in Settings, use "Clear history" or uninstall the app.

8. Changes

If data handling changes we will update this page and show the new date.
